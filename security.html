Security and data handling

What the connector can reach, what it keeps, and where

A plain-language account of how aPlan4Sheets moves data between Anaplan and Google Sheets, written for the people who review add-ons before a finance team installs them. The Privacy Policy and Terms of Service are the governing documents; this page explains them.

Last reviewed: October 29, 2026

At a glance

Your Anaplan permissions apply

Every request to Anaplan carries your own credentials. The connector can only read the workspaces, models, views and cells you can already open in Anaplan.

Read from Anaplan, write to your sheet

Data flows one way: from Anaplan into the Google Sheet you chose. The connector does not write to Anaplan models. Exporting an AI result to a sheet is a separate, explicit step.

Runs on Google infrastructure

The sidebar is a Google Apps Script add-on. Heavier work runs in a connector service in the aPlan4Sheets Google Cloud project (us-central1). There is no third-party hosting.

Outbound requests are allow-listed

The add-on manifest limits the hosts it may call to Anaplan's authentication and regional API endpoints and the connector's own Google Cloud services.

Architecture

Three parts, all on Google infrastructure: the sidebar inside Sheets, the connector service that does the heavy lifting, and the Anaplan APIs. AI features add a fourth hop that only runs when you ask a question.

Authentication to Anaplan

  • OAuth 2.0 / SSO is the recommended path. A tenant security administrator creates an OAuth client in Anaplan Administration and the user signs in through Anaplan's own login page; the connector never sees the password. The OAuth client walkthrough shows the administrator steps. Organizational accounts only.
  • Basic Auth is available where SSO is not. If you choose it, your Anaplan email and password are kept in your own user-specific Apps Script storage so sessions can be renewed. They are not shared with other users and are cleared when you sign out.
  • Tokens expire on Anaplan's schedule. Signing out clears the interactive tokens or credentials for the current method. Schedules keep a separate authentication record so they can run while you are away; deleting the schedule removes it.

Scheduled refreshes

A schedule is a time-based Google trigger that runs as the user who created it, on an hourly, daily or weekly cadence. Timing is approximate because Google manages the trigger, and a run needs valid Anaplan authentication and an available source model. Status and errors appear in the sidebar; email notifications are opt-in. Deleting the schedule stops it and removes its definition and authentication record.

AI features

  • What is sent. When you ask the AI Analyst a question, the connector sends your question together with the model metadata or query results needed to answer it. Nothing is sent until you ask.
  • Where it is processed. The AI Analyst runs on Google Cloud Vertex AI (Gemini models) inside the connector's own Google Cloud project, under Google Cloud's Vertex AI terms. The Claude handoff is an optional pilot: it is available only where a customer configures the Claude connector, and it sends the selected context to Anthropic's Claude when you trigger that separate action.
  • Review before export. Answers come back to the sidebar for you to read. A result reaches your sheet only when you export it, and no AI feature writes to Anaplan.
  • What is recorded. Usage metadata such as token counts is kept with the operational records described above.

Transport, hosting and this website

  • All traffic between the sidebar, the connector service, Anaplan and Google APIs uses HTTPS.
  • The connector service runs on Google App Engine and Cloud Run in us-central1, with logs in Cloud Logging for diagnostics.
  • Anaplan's regional endpoints are supported, so a model hosted in the EU, Canada or Asia-Pacific is read from its own region.
  • This website is static, served over HTTPS, sets no cookies, runs no analytics or trackers, and contains no customer data. Every demo is a simulation with fictional data.

Your controls

  • Sign out in the sidebar to clear the current method's tokens or credentials.
  • Disable or delete schedules in the sidebar and check the confirmation.
  • Uninstall from Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons, then request deletion of anything retained.
  • Report a concern to ryan.domke@aplan4sheets.com. Security reports are read by the developer directly.

Compliance

The add-on complies with Google Workspace Marketplace policies and the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Connector data is never used for advertising or sold. Read the Privacy Policy for the full retention and deletion terms and the Terms of Service for acceptable use.
