Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 27, 2026

Overview

aPlan4Sheets ("the Add-on", formerly listed as Anaplan Connector for Sheets™) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy describes how we handle your data when you use our Google Sheets™ add-on.

Information We Access

  • Google Sheets™ Data: Spreadsheet content and configuration used for the reports, refreshes, and workbook copies you request or schedule
  • Anaplan Authentication: OAuth tokens and configured OAuth client information; if you choose Basic Auth, your Anaplan email and password are also retained to authenticate and renew sessions
  • User Email: Used for account identification, authentication, support, first-party product usage analysis, and enabled export notifications

Information We Store

  • Configuration Data: Your Anaplan workspace/model selections, stored in Google Properties Service
  • Schedule Settings: Workbook-specific schedule definitions are stored in Google Apps Script Document Properties. Execution metadata is also retained in user-specific Apps Script storage and the Google-hosted scheduling service.
  • Authentication Records: Interactive authentication state is retained in Google Apps Script storage. Schedules can retain separate authentication records in workbook-specific Apps Script storage and the Google-hosted execution service.
  • Server Processing: Google-hosted services process Anaplan queries and write results to the selected Google Sheet
  • Operational Records: Account email/domain and usage metadata are retained in our Google Cloud project for support and product usage analysis. These are not third-party advertising records.

How We Use Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Authenticate with Anaplan on your behalf
  • Execute data exports from Anaplan to Google Sheets™
  • Run the scheduled exports you enable; timing is approximate and depends on authentication and source availability
  • Send notification emails about export status (only if enabled)
  • Provide requested AI analysis and model exploration
  • Diagnose service failures and understand first-party product usage

Data Sharing

Data is processed by Google infrastructure to provide the connector. When you invoke an AI feature, the selected prompt and relevant context or query results may be processed by the configured AI provider. Claude handoffs are optional, require a configured connector, and involve a separate user action.

We do not use connector data for advertising or sell it to advertisers.

Data Security

  • Requests to Anaplan and the Google-hosted connector services use HTTPS
  • Authentication records are held in the Google-hosted storage described above
  • Anaplan access depends on your account's model and data permissions
  • The connector supports OAuth/SSO and Basic Auth, subject to your organization's configuration

Data Retention

  • Saved connection and schedule configurations are retained in Google Apps Script storage until removed through the relevant controls
  • Authentication tokens expire according to Anaplan's policies
  • Logout clears the current sign-in method's interactive tokens or Basic Auth credentials. It does not remove saved OAuth client settings, schedules, separate schedule authentication records, or server-side operational usage records.
  • To stop scheduled refreshes, disable or delete each schedule and check the result. A successful schedule deletion stops its execution and removes its local schedule definition and schedule authentication record; this is not a promise to erase every server-side operational record.
  • Uninstalling or ending use is not an automatic deletion request for all retained authentication or configuration data. Contact us to request deletion of retained records.
  • Legacy usage records and aggregate account profiles do not currently have automatic expiry. Uninstalling the add-on does not delete these records; contact us to request their deletion.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Remove the add-on at any time (Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons)
  • Sign out of the current interactive authentication session using the logout function
  • Disable or delete scheduled exports through the add-on interface and check the confirmation
  • Request data deletion by contacting us at ryan.domke@aplan4sheets.com

Google API Services User Data Policy

This add-on complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes to This Policy
