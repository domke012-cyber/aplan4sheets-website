Fluence XL / AXL and Google Sheets: where aPlan4Sheets complements Anaplan XL

Fluence XL is now Anaplan XL Reporting (AXL), Anaplan's own add-in for Excel. If part of your team reports from Google Workspace instead, here is how a browser-native Google Sheets connector fits alongside it.

Start with the workflow, not the tool name

Fluence XL and AXL are the names most people still use for Anaplan XL, Anaplan's Excel-centred reporting add-in. For many teams, Excel is exactly the right environment, and Anaplan XL is the supported way to work there. For teams that live in Google Workspace, the requirement is different: keep Anaplan as the planning source, but make reporting, review, and collaboration happen in Google Sheets.

aPlan4Sheets is built for that second workflow. It connects Anaplan models to Google Sheets so teams can run exports and saved views, build pivots, collaborate in a shared sheet, and refresh reports from the browser. It does not replace Anaplan XL for the Excel side; it covers the Google Sheets side of the same reporting need.

When a Google Sheets workflow is worth evaluating

  • Your finance team already builds recurring reporting packs in Google Sheets.
  • Mac or Chromebook users need direct access to Anaplan-connected spreadsheets.
  • Business partners review numbers, comments, and follow-ups in shared Google files.
  • You want scheduled refreshes for board reports, dashboards, or weekly variance packs.
  • You want to reduce manual export, paste, and reformat steps between Anaplan and Sheets.

Where aPlan4Sheets fits

aPlan4Sheets is a practical fit when the desired output is a Google Sheet connected to Anaplan. It is designed for browser-based setup, shared workbooks, Anaplan pivots, export actions and saved views, supported UX grid formatting, and scheduled refreshes with status feedback in the sidebar.

If your reporting estate depends on existing Excel workbooks, macros, or Microsoft 365 controls, Anaplan XL remains the right home for those reports. Many organizations run both: Anaplan XL for Excel users and aPlan4Sheets for Google Workspace users, with Anaplan as the single source for each. A clean decision reflects the platform each team actually uses and the reports that already exist.

Suggested evaluation path

  1. Pick one recurring report that currently starts with an Anaplan export.
  2. Identify the export actions, saved views, or pivots that feed the report.
  3. Rebuild the source tabs in Google Sheets with aPlan4Sheets.
  4. Add formulas, charts, commentary, and sharing controls around the connected data.
  5. Add a schedule, then check its status in the sidebar before the next reporting cycle.
